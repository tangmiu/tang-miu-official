BIO

Tang Miu is an independent indie pop artist and musician who began his music journey in 2017.

Working largely by himself, he writes, records and produces his own music. His songs explore feelings, memories, relationships and everyday experiences, turning personal moments into an ongoing musical diary.

His work spans songwriting, recording, production, live performance and visual work around his releases. Each song is another page in an evolving record of where he was and what he was becoming.
